Chicken Soup Receipe

Ingredients

  • 500g Chicken
  • 4 Cups water
  • 1 Onion
  • 2 Carrots
  • 2 Garlic Cloves
  • Salt according to your taste
  • Black peeper

Steps Of Cooking

  1. Wash the chicken
  2. Boil the chicken with water.
  3. Add onions,carrots,and garlic.
  4. Add salt and black pepper.
  5. Cook for 30-40 minutes.
  6. Serve hot.
